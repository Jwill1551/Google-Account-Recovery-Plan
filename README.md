# Google Account Recovery & Protection Guide

A practical toolkit for recovering a hacked Google account and protecting the people who use it. It's based on a real freelance IT support case: a client lost his Gmail to a phishing email, and his wife's passwords showed up in a dark web leak. To see the live word document click [`https://docs.google.com/document/d 1cohPrnRM3Y7NMsN_1WDs4jfwqz0zDVK6tFOzj0CCXFA/edit?usp=sharing`](here).

The toolkit has three parts, each for a different reader:

| File | Who it's for | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`technician-runbook.md`](technician-runbook.md) | IT technicians and help desk staff | Step-by-step checklist for recovering and securing a compromised Google account |

## The problem

Most people treat a hacked account as fixed once they change the password. Attackers often leave ways back in: forwarding rules, Gmail filters, connected apps, or their own recovery phone. Meanwhile the victim may be losing money through gift card fraud, while their contacts get phished from their address.

This toolkit covers the whole incident: getting back in, removing what the attacker left behind, hardening the account, protecting the client's money and identity, and teaching them how not to get caught again.

## How to use it

**If you're a technician:**

1. Open [`technician-runbook.md`](technician-runbook.md) on a trusted device and sit with the client.
2. Work through sections 0–6 in order and tick off each item.
3. Give the client a printed copy of the quick guide before you leave.

**If you're a user:** print [`google-account-quick-guide.pdf`](google-account-quick-guide.pdf) and keep it somewhere you'll find it. If you think you've been hacked, follow the red section first.

## What the runbook covers

- **Regain control:** recover the account, reset the password, sign out unknown devices
- **Remove persistence:** check recovery info, 2SV methods, third-party apps, app passwords, filters, forwarding, delegates and Google Pay
- **Harden:** passkeys, 2SV without text messages, backup codes, Enhanced Safe Browsing, Security Checkup
- **Protect beyond Google:** card disputes, password reuse, credit freezes, IdentityTheft.gov, SIM lock PIN
- **Educate:** walk the client through the phishing email they fell for, and the "don't sign in from an email link" rule
- **Close out:** record what you found and schedule a follow-up

It also covers **Google Workspace** accounts, which need an admin-side review in addition to the user steps.
